Saudade

noun · Portuguese · saved

Canonical: saudade

Translation

Deep emotional longing or nostalgia

Explanation

A melancholic longing for something beautiful that is absent — a person, a place, a feeling once known. Often described as untranslatable, it sits somewhere between grief, love, and memory.

Examples

"It is the quiet ache of something once loved and now gone."

"He left Portugal but saudade followed him everywhere."

"Fado music is the sound of saudade made audible."
